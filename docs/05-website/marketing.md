# Marketing and Go to Market

Source: MaxBid Project Workbook, tab 37 Marketing.

## Channels

**Organic search.** Value guides, calculators, auction house guides and glossary. Long term primary channel.

**AI answers.** Methodology, accuracy and answer first content per tab 36.

**Communities.** Reseller Facebook groups, YouTube, Reddit and trade forums. Give real answers and breakdowns. Follow each group's rules. No spam.

**Email.** Weekly Auction Breakdown newsletter. Owned audience from day one.

**Referral.** Both organisations receive bonus credits after the referred organisation's first payment.

**Paid.** Deferred until conversion rates are proven. Then retargeting and branded search first.

## Auction Breakdown format

**Cadence.** Weekly, published midweek before weekend closes.

**Content.** One upcoming catalogue. Top ten lots by opportunity score with estimate range, confidence, rough max bid and a link to the lot.

**Outputs.** Web page, newsletter, short social clips of the lots and figures, community post summary.

**Rules.** No copied photos or listing text. Standard disclaimers. Figures from triage clearly labelled as rough.

**Call to action.** Run your own numbers on these lots with a free account.

## Launch plan

**Now to Week 1.** Lock name and domains. Holding page with waitlist live.

**Build weeks.** Share build progress and early breakdowns with the waitlist.

**Month 3.** Marketing site, try before sign up, first four breakdowns.

**Month 4.** Founding member offer to the waitlist (O14), then public launch.

**After launch.** Value guides roll out as data thresholds are met.

## Lifecycle email

| Email | Trigger | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome | Sign up | Three step setup and first analysis prompt |
| First result | Triage complete | What your top lots mean and how to shortlist |
| Nudge | No deep analysis after 3 days | Try one lot free using starter credits |
| Closing soon | Watchlist trigger | Transactional alert with target and maximum |
| Outcome reminder | Lot closed | Record the result to sharpen future estimates |
| Monthly recap | Monthly | Lots analysed, won, margin achieved |
| Win back | 30 days inactive | Latest breakdown and what is new |
